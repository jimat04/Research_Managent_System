# Citation audit for Chapters 1 and 2

Reviewed 20 October 2026. Source: `rms-chapter_1&2.docx`.

This audit covers all 26 original bibliography entries and the institutional manual cited in the body but omitted from the original bibliography. It distinguishes bibliographic checks from access to full text. An inaccessible DOI, an unsuccessful search, or the absence of an authoritative match is **not** proof that a publication is fabricated. Unresolved material is removed from the revision rather than presented as verified research.

The revised manuscript contains 16 references. Five foreign/foundational discussions and two verified Philippine studies replace the original unsupported study summaries. A local institutional-context discussion is identified separately from empirical research. The revision does not preserve an arbitrary count of five Philippine empirical studies by inventing replacements.
