## Changes from the preceding revision
- Ten older or undated entries were removed from the preceding 16-entry bibliography; six dated sources were added, leaving 12. Recent reviews support the acceptance discussion without assigning them authorship of the original theory.
- The planned quality framework now identifies ISO/IEC 25010:2023. The questions, objectives, selected quality discussion, definitions and framework use Interaction Capability consistently. The instrument still needs review before data collection.
- Undated product/manual pages remain useful for implementation research, but are excluded from the recent-reference bibliography. A retrieval year is not a publication year.
- The 2015 institutional manual is excluded and recorded separately in [the institutional source exception note](institutional-source-exception.md). No exception or newer edition is assumed.
