# Research Manual 2015 Reference

This project uses the EARIST Research Manual 2015 as the functional guide for research workflow, roles, documents, and milestone tracking.

## Manual-Aligned Workflow

1. Proposal Submission
2. CREC Evaluation
3. ORS Consolidation
4. EREC Research Forum
5. Approval, Revision, or Disapproval
6. President Approval
7. MOU and NDA completion
8. Research Implementation
9. Midway Progress Report
10. Terminal Report Review
11. Final Bound Report
12. Research Colloquium
13. Archive and Publication Tracking

## Standard Five-Chapter Structure

### Chapter 1: The Problem and Its Background

- Background of the Study
- Statement of the Problem
- General and Specific Objectives
- Scope and Limitations
- Significance of the Study
- Definition of Terms

### Chapter 2: Review of Related Literature

- Foreign Literature
- Local Literature
- Foreign Studies
- Local Studies
- Theoretical Framework
- Conceptual Framework

### Chapter 3: Research Methodology

- Research Design
- Respondents
- Research Instruments
- Data Gathering Procedure
- Statistical Treatment

### Chapter 4: Presentation, Analysis, and Interpretation of Data

- Findings
- Data Presentation
- Analysis
- Interpretation

### Chapter 5: Summary of Findings, Conclusions, and Recommendations

- Summary of Findings
- Conclusions
- Recommendations

## Core Roles

- Student researcher or student research team
- Faculty adviser
- CREC reviewers
- EREC reviewers
- Research office staff
- Research coordinator or research head
- Administrator

## Required Documents

- Proposal document
- Chapter 1 to Chapter 5 submissions
- Revision checklist or reviewer feedback
- Defense or forum materials
- Memorandum of Research Undertaking
- Non-Disclosure Agreement
- Midway progress report
- Terminal report
- Final bound report
- Publication or archive record

## Project Implications

- Research records should track CREC and EREC review stages separately.
- Chapter submissions should enforce the five-chapter structure.
- Faculty review actions should support approval, revision, and feedback history.
- Research implementation should include progress and terminal report milestones.
- Archive and publication views should distinguish completed, colloquium-ready, and published research.

## Current Implementation Notes

- Chapter upload pages already use the five-chapter structure.
- Faculty review already moves projects through submitted, CREC review, EREC review, and approved statuses.
- CSRF protection is now present on state-changing forms.
- Future work should prioritize missing manual workflow items: MOU/NDA tracking, progress reports, terminal reports, colloquium scheduling, and publication status.
