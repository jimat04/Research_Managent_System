# RMS Specification (from Research Manual)

## Chapter Structure
Thesis/Capstone projects must have exactly 5 chapters:
1. The Problem and Its Background
   - Background of the Study
   - Statement of the Problem
   - Objectives (General + Specific)
   - Scope and Limitations
   - Significance of the Study
   - Definition of Terms
2. Review of Related Literature
   - Foreign & Local Literature
   - Foreign & Local Studies
   - Theoretical Framework
   - Conceptual Framework
3. Research Methodology
   - Research Design
   - Respondents
   - Instruments
   - Data Gathering Procedure
   - Statistical Treatment
4. Presentation, Analysis and Interpretation of Data
   - Findings
   - Analysis
5. Summary of Findings, Conclusions, and Recommendations

## Status / Approval Workflow
1. Draft → student creates proposal
2. Submitted for proposal defense
3. Proposal defense: scheduled in defense_schedule
4. Revised proposal (if with corrections)
5. Defense proper (final)
6. Approved / Completed
7. Archived

## Required Roles
- Student Author(s) — multiple per project via project_members
- Thesis Adviser (faculty) — assigned per project
- Panel Members (faculty, 2-3 per defense)
- Research Coordinator (admin)
- Department Head (admin approval)

## Required Uploads
- Proposal Document (PDF/DOCX)
- Chapter 1-5 submissions (DOCX/PDF)
- Defense slides (PPTX/PDF)
- Final Manuscript (PDF, signed)
- Approval Sheet (signed/scanned PDF)

## Grading / Scoring
- Panel scores: 1-100 per panelist per criteria
- Criteria: (list from the manual)
- Passing: 75+
- Best thesis: 90+

## Academic Year / Semester Rules
- Projects are tied to an academic year and semester
- Cannot enroll in Thesis 2 without passing Thesis 1 (not tracked in v1)
